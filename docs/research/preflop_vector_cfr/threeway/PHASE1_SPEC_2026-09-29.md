## 0. Summary

1. **The tree is fully explained by one rule set.** A pot-sized first raise (100 % of the pot after the
   call) is followed only by all-in re-raises, the all-in is available at every decision that does not
   face an all-in, and limps, over-limps, cold calls and multiway calls of an all-in are all allowed.
   Under this rule set the oracle rebuilds **exactly** the 54 files: the same 54 paths with no file
   missing and none extra, and the same action set at every node (0 mismatches). The same rule also
   rebuilds the HU50 set (8 files).
2. **The only engine gap is the size of the first raise.** The first-raise amounts are 6a when UTG
   opens, 6a when CO opens after the UTG folds, 7a when CO isolates over the UTG limp, 6a when BTN raises
   over one limp and 7a when BTN raises over two limps. The engine only has fixed live targets
   (`open_target_units`), so it cannot produce these amounts: a 6a target gives 20 missing and 20 extra
   files, 7a gives 16/16, and {6a, 7a} gives 90 nodes (36 extra). Everything else is already supported by
   `game_model` and `compiled_game` for 3 players: multiway fold, limp flag, aggression levels,
   unconditional all-in and the donk switch.
3. **The fix is small.** One optional config key, `preflop_open_sizes_basis_points` (for example
   `[10000]`), makes level 0 use the core's percentage sizes instead of targets. The core already
   implements Monker's pot-raise arithmetic (`legal_actions`, game.cpp:392-396 and 446-483). HU
   fingerprints do not change because the key is serialized only when present.
4. **The chart naming needs one rule for multiway.** MonkerSolver leaves a fold out of the file name
   when it is the player's first action (UTG_6.0ante_BTN means UTG raises, CO folds, BTN acts) and
   writes later folds (…_UTG_Fold_CO). Heads-up names are unchanged by this rule.
5. **Predicted size**, with postflop rules G1 (one size of 100 % pot, donk bets allowed, no all-in cap)
   on the compact abstraction (15x4 + TX2):
   - **7,225 nodes**, of which 54 preflop decisions and 330/936/1,802 flop/turn/river decisions.
   - **847,242,189 cells**: 13.56 GB in double or 6.78 GB in float32 for regrets plus strategy sums, plus
     0.77 GB of lazy-discount timestamps.
   - With the 5x all-in cap: 7,126 nodes and 835,014,699 cells (13.36 GB double). The cap saves 1.4 %.
   - The three-way limped pot alone is 47 % of all cells, and the four three-way flop entries together
     are 77 %.

## 2. MonkerSolver's preflop rules, as read from the files

The money convention is the HU50 one (`benchmarks/monker/HU50.json`):
- `ante_units` 10000: every player posts a dead ante of 1a.
- `button_blind_units` 10000: the BTN posts a live blind of 1a.
- `effective_stack_units` 500000: the stack is 50a *including* the ante, so 49a is live.
- The root pot is 4a (3 antes plus the blind). The first actor completes for 1a.

**Pot and blind check.** The five raise amounts check the pot and the blind: every one equals
`current bet + (pot + call)`, which only holds with a root pot of 4a and a live blind of 1a.

| Situation | Pot before | Call | Raise to (live) | Increment / pot after call |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UTG open (#1) | 4 | 1 | 6.0 | 5 / 5 = 100 % |
| CO open after the UTG folds (#2) | 4 | 1 | 6.0 | 5 / 5 = 100 % |
| CO isolation over the UTG limp (#5) | 5 | 1 | 7.0 | 6 / 6 = 100 % |
| BTN raise over the UTG limp, CO folded (#11) | 5 | 0 | 6.0 (bet 5 on top of its 1a blind) | 5 / 5 = 100 % |
| BTN raise over the CO limp, UTG folded (#8) | 5 | 0 | 6.0 | 5 / 5 = 100 % |
| BTN raise over two limps (#26) | 6 | 0 | 7.0 | 6 / 6 = 100 % |
| HU50 CO open / BTN isolation (same rule, 2 players) | 3 / 4 | 1 / 0 | 5.0 / 5.0 | 4/4, 4/4 = 100 % |

**Rules:**
1. **Order.** Preflop action runs UTG, CO, BTN. The BTN acts last and holds the option (it can
   `Check` behind limps and never has a Fold there).
2. **Limp.** UTG may open-limp (`Call` 1a). CO may over-limp behind UTG, or open-limp after the UTG
   folds. The BTN cannot limp (it is the blind).
3. **First raise.** Exactly one sized raise per hand, at 100 % of the pot. It is available at
   aggression level 0 to UTG, CO and BTN, i.e. the open, the isolation over any number of limpers, and
   the BTN raise over limps.
4. **Re-raise.** After the first raise the only re-raise is the all-in (level 1: Fold/Call/AllIn). There
   is no 3-bet size and no 4-bet size. A limper facing a raise may limp-call or limp-shove (#14, #16, #30,
   #32, #43, #44).
5. **All-in.** The all-in is offered at every decision that does not face an all-in (open-shove,
   shove over limps, shove over a raise, BTN shove on its option). Facing an all-in the only actions are
   Call and Fold (level 1 or 2). Stacks are equal, so every call of an all-in is itself all-in. There
   are no side pots and no call for less.
6. **Cold call.** Cold calls are allowed everywhere:
   - CO flats the UTG open (#3, 0.1 %).
   - BTN over-calls the open plus a call (#22, 0.3 %).
   - BTN calls the CO isolation (#24).
   - Players call after a limp-raise (#43).
   - Multiway calls of an all-in are allowed (#23, #27, #37, #39, #47, #49, #53).

   None of Monker's multiway "no cold call when x players are left to act" filters is in use in this
   tree.
7. **Raise cap.** In effect there is one sized raise followed by all-ins, so the tree ends at level 2
   (sized raise, then all-in, then call/fold) or at level 1 (all-in first).
8. **Sparse action sets.** Nothing is pruned per node or per position. Every node's action set follows
   from rules 1-7.

The HU50 set follows the same rules; there they are expressed by `open_target_units [50000]` and
`response_target_units []`.

## 3. Ambiguities and flags

- **A1: fold omission in file names** (naming only, not tree shape).
  - Omitted folds are always the folder's first action of the hand:
    - UTG root fold, giving `CO/CO_strategy.txt`.
    - CO fold after the UTG action, giving `BTN/UTG_6.0ante_BTN_…`.
    - BTN fold as its first action, giving `UTG/UTG_6.0ante_CO_AllIn_UTG_…` (BTN faced a shove) and
      `UTG/UTG_Call_CO_7.0ante_UTG_…` (BTN faced the isolation).
  - In all, 19 of the 54 names contain at least one omitted fold.
  - Written folds are always a second action: `…_UTG_Fold_CO` (UTG had limped or opened), `…_CO_Fold_BTN`
    (CO had limped or raised), `…_BTN_Fold_UTG` (BTN had raised).
  - An alternative reading, "omit folds of players without voluntary chips in", gives the same names on
    this tree, because every later fold here comes after a voluntary call or raise. The two readings
    could only differ in trees where a player checks and folds preflop, which cannot happen.
  - Names stay unique: the players skipped during the first orbit are exactly the omitted folders. The
    tool should still assert uniqueness.
- **A2: first-raise sizing rule.** "100 % pot" fits all 6 amounts with one parameter. Per-situation
  fixed sizes (6/7/6/6/7) would give the same tree. The two readings only diverge for other stacks or
  antes, not for this tree.
- **A3: why only all-in after the first raise.** It could be "no re-raise size configured" or "a
  pot-sized re-raise converted to all-in by an all-in threshold". A pot 3-bet over 6a would be 22a (45 %
  of the stack), so a threshold at or below about 45 % would convert it. The files cannot tell the two
  apart. The difference only matters for deeper stacks. For HU50 we already chose `response []`.
- **A4: stack convention (50a including the ante, 49a live).** File names never show all-in amounts,
  so the 3-way files cannot confirm it. It is the HU50 convention that reproduced Monker's equity-driven
  HU nodes (95-98 % the same action). If Monker meant 50a behind the ante, every all-in would be 1a
  larger (about a 2 % change in pot odds at the call/fold-versus-shove nodes). Tree unaffected.
- **A5: rake.** 5 %, cap 3a, no flop no drop, per the user of 29/09 23:00. It is invisible in the tree
  and changes only payoffs. Use the rake fixture once the rake commit lands (section 4.2).
- **A6: near-unreached lines** (risk for phase 3, not phase 1). UTG opens 6a with 0.1 % (#1), CO flats
  the open with 0.1 % (#3), and BTN over-calls with 0.3 % (#22). The subtrees behind the UTG open (#3, #9,
  #12, #13, #21, #22, #27, #28, #37, #38) are nearly off-path in Monker's solution. This is the same
  off-path problem HU50 showed on "BTN vs open" (Monker opens 0.5 %). Chart distances on those nodes
  will be noisy.
- **A7: postflop rules.** The files say nothing about postflop. Per the task, we use HU50 G1: one size
  of 100 % pot for bet and raise, all-in always, donk bets allowed, no cap. Monker's own multiway
  guidance (no donk, cold-call filters) would shrink the tree. The no-donk variant was measured by the
  oracle: 5,817 nodes and 664 M cells (section 6.4).

    [--write-manifest <tsv>] [--json <report>]

Steps:
1. Parse the config and run `CompiledGame::compile(config, {.preflop_only = true})`.
2. Get `mc::chart_nodes(game)` (with the 1c fold rule), giving the expected `relative` paths, the tokens

   - from `--charts`: `recursive_directory_iterator` over `*_strategy.txt` with `generic_string()`

     expected names.
   - **C2 actions.** For every common node, the header columns equal the tokens in `chart.columns`

   - **C3 classes** (`--charts` only). The row labels equal `mc::hand_classes()` labels (81), every
     frequency is ≥ 0, and every total is within 0.002 of 0 or 1.
   - **C4 pot rule** (pot mode only). For every Bet/Raise edge, `(payment − to_call) / (pot + to_call)`
     equals the configured size within ±1 unit, and the token amount equals the child's
     `committed_this_street[actor]`.
   - **C5 summary.** Decisions per actor, action-signature histogram, the preflop stats of
     `CompiledGameStats` (decisions, folds, runouts, entries with the active-player count of each
     entry). Per node: pot, to-call, level, `limped_pot`, live commitments.
5. Output: JSON report (`schema gtosd.preflop_blueprint_monker_tree_report.v1`) and a last line

### K2. Inferences about MonkerSolver's rules: evidence the spec did not use

   - **This settles A3.** At 100a a pot 3-bet over the 6a open would go to 22a, 22 % of the 99a live stack.

2. **The 40a and 60a folders are byte-identical** (`diff -rq` is empty, both dated 11/08/2026). Their Call_EV
   values fit a **60a** stack, not 40a (`ev_convention_probe.py`):
   - AA calling the UTG shove on `BTN/UTG_AllIn_BTN`: Monker gives 26.233 a. A 40a stack predicts 16.5-19.1 a,
     a 60a stack 25.4-27.6 a.

3. **The 60a set writes amounts differently.** Its tokens are 7/8/8/9 instead of 6/7/6/7, so a raw name check
   fails (36 missing / 36 extra). The rule itself is not different: the Fold_EV columns give the live amounts.
   - How to read Fold_EV: a fold's EV is minus everything the player has put in, antes included (UTG root −1,
     BTN facing a shove −2, a limper −2).
   - The UTG open "7.0ante" has Fold_EV −7, so its live amount is 6. The CO isolation "8.0ante" has −8, so
     live 7. The BTN raise "8.0ante" over one limp has −7, so live 6. The BTN raise "9.0ante" over two limps
     has −8, so live 7.

     called the CO's 8.0ante has −8.
   - So the live amounts are exactly the pot rule's. Only the token convention differs: UTG/CO token = live +
     1a, BTN token = live + 2a.
   - Three independent solves (50a, 100a, 60a) follow the same rule.
   - If the 60a set is ever used as a tree reference, the 1d tool needs a token-offset option. Until then it
     must report unknown tokens explicitly, not as silent mismatches.
